Field notes

Photography is where I first learned to notice small changes in what I was looking at.

I've photographed since 2018, mostly plants, insects and birds in the places I've lived: Gurgaon and Delhi, and now New York. Most of it is slow work, waiting for a bee to settle or for the light to move across a leaf. The same habits carry into research: watching closely, staying patient with a subject that won't hold still, and noticing the detail that is easy to miss.

Every photo here is unedited, as it came out of the camera. I've always been a little camera shy, which is probably why I'm more comfortable behind one.
